# research-planning

- **research-planning** — the front end of research: pick the method that actually answers your question, then draft the instrument — interview/discussion guides, usability-test scripts (tasks + scenarios), survey questionnaires, and recruiting screeners. Writes non-leading questions and builds in consent and realistic sample sizes.

**Project-agnostic:** nothing about the domain or product is baked in — the skill starts from your research question and audience and adapts. Pairs directly with the `ux-research` plugin's `research-synthesis`, which takes the data you collect and turns it back into themes.

No external services required. Triggers from natural language ("write an interview guide", "plan a usability test", "design a survey", "how should I research this?").

Install: `/plugin install research-planning@ai-ux-toolkit`
